Ad Shield settings

General

Enabled

Master switch for everything below, on all sites and on Twitch. Open tabs need a reload.

Debug logging

Logs details with the prefix [TAS] in the page and extension consoles.

All sites

Ads

Blocks ad requests and hides ad elements (EasyList).

Fix YouTube ads and anti-adblock walls (page scripts)

Removes ad data from YouTube's player and feed responses before the page uses it, and skips an ad that still plays. Runs a small built-in script inside YouTube pages only; needs Ads on.

Trackers

Blocks tracking and analytics requests (EasyPrivacy).

Strip tracking cookies

Removes cookies from third-party requests to known tracker domains (taken from EasyPrivacy) and drops the cookies they try to set.

Cookie banners: hide

Hides cookie notices and blocks consent-banner scripts (EasyList Cookie List).

Cookie banners: auto-reject

Clicks "reject" / "only necessary" on known consent pop-ups for you (DuckDuckGo autoconsent). Adds a script to every page and frame, so it is off by default.

Sites where blocking is off

One site per line, for example example.com (covers its subdomains). Nothing is blocked or hidden there, including the Twitch features on twitch.tv. The popup's site switch edits this list too. Reload open tabs after a change.

    My filters

    Elements you hid with "Hide an element on this page" in the popup, one per line as example.com##.selector (the site covers its subdomains; a.com,b.com##.selector for several sites). You can add, edit or delete lines here. Lines starting with ! are comments and are not kept. Filters apply on their sites whenever Ad Shield is on there, also with Ads switched off; not on sites where blocking is off. Per site only: a line without a site is rejected. Reload open tabs after a change.

      Twitch

      Block client-side ad requests

      Blocks edge.ads.twitch.tv and the Amazon ad script on Twitch (network rules).

      Block ad tracking paths

      Blocks twitch.tv/r/s/ and /r/c/ requests. Off by default; untested on live Twitch.

      Hide standalone video ads

      Hides, mutes and pauses separate ad videos beside the player or in chat.

      Prefer AVC (H.264) streams

      Removes HEVC/AV1 variants when an H.264 one exists, which avoids player errors when switching streams during ads. Turn off to keep 1440p/4K "enhanced" qualities.

      Show a notice when an ad is blocked

      A small "Ad blocked – using backup stream" note in the player.

      Twitch backup stream order

      When a stream has an ad, these player types are asked for an ad-free copy, in this order. One per line. Known types: . "autoplay" (360p) must be last.
